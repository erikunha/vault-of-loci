# OpenReview

> [!tldr] In 3 lines
> A platform where conferences and journals open submissions and reviews.
> The home page lists news and active venues, among them TMLR and DMLR.
> Useful to see the review state of an ML paper.

## Study explanation

The home page shows three blocks. News, with items from 2026 such as multi-factor authentication for all users (23 March 2026) and a survey on support for more openness in AI peer review (13 February 2026). Active venues, such as TMLR, Computo, DMLR and MELBA. And venues open for submissions, such as AAAI 2027 workshops.

The page lists venues and their events, which shows that OpenReview is a place where peer review happens. To validate a paper: look for it in its venue and see whether it was accepted. The home page does not say how to see the reviews, decisions or comments. Citation counts are not a stated function here.

The home page mentions multi-factor authentication for all users. Whether reading needs an account is not verified. The page states no cost.

## Key takeaways

- **Remember:** OpenReview is where you look for the review state of a paper: find the venue, then see if the paper was accepted.
- **Common confusion:** Being listed in a venue is not the same as being accepted. Check the paper's status in the venue.
- **Exercise:** Name three venues from the home page and say which ones were open for submissions.
