## Study explanation

The home page invites you to discover the world of scientific literature. It says the features can be viewed on the page, but that creating a Litmap needs a larger screen. It states "Used by 900,000+ researchers worldwide". The menus are About, Features, Pricing, Company and Blog.

The name and the invitation suggest exploring by maps, but the extracted page does not explain how the map works or whether it shows citations. So gaps, citations, peer review and recency are not verified. Open the Features page (https://www.litmaps.com/features) in a browser.

Account or key: not verified. There is a Pricing menu, but its content was not read.

## Key takeaways

- **Remember:** Litmaps is a map tool for literature. How the map works is not verified: read its Features page.
- **Common confusion:** A user count on a vendor page is the vendor's claim. It does not show that the maps are good.
