# ACL Anthology

> [!tldr] In 3 lines
> A library of publications in computational linguistics and speech and natural language processing.
> It hosts papers of the official venues of the ACL and of other organizations.
> On the page: 139,573 papers, 3,563 volumes, 548 venues and 75 years.

## Study explanation

The page defines the ACL Anthology as a library of publications in the fields of computational linguistics and of speech and natural language processing. It hosts papers of the official venues of the Association for Computational Linguistics and of other organizations. The numbers shown on 2026-11-21: 139,573 papers, 149,638 authors, 3,563 volumes, 548 venues and 75 years.

For an AI student, it is the reference source for NLP papers, such as those of ACL conferences. Being hosted there shows publication in a listed venue, which helps to validate publication status. The page does not speak of peer review. The home page also lists links by author and by venue. Citations, recency and gaps: not verified.

The page mentions no account and no key. Whether there is an API: not verified. The page states no cost.

## Key takeaways

- **Remember:** For NLP papers, the ACL Anthology shows that a paper was published in a listed venue.
- **Common confusion:** Published in a venue is not the same as peer reviewed. The page does not say how the venues review.
- **Exercise:** Find one NLP paper you know. Write its venue and year as the Anthology lists them.
