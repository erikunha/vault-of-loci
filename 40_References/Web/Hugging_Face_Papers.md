# Hugging Face Papers

> [!tldr] In 3 lines
> A list of trending papers with a short summary and a publication date.
> A daily email of trending papers is offered, through login.
> Good for recency. It does not replace checking citations and peer review.

## Study explanation

The page lists papers with a title, a summary of one or two sentences, a publication date and who submitted them. Some items show the author's organization. A notice offers the trending papers in your inbox once a day, with a Subscribe button that leads to login.

For recency, the list carries publication dates (for example, items from November 2026), so it shows what the community is reading now. An earlier check, in a private guide, recorded Daily, Weekly and Monthly tabs and that arXiv is the only supported source. Those tabs did not appear in this extraction. Citations and peer review: not verified. Use it as a radar, not as validation.

Viewing the list needed no login. Subscribing to the daily email leads to a login page. The page states no price.

## Key takeaways

- **Remember:** Hugging Face Papers is a radar for what is new. Validate each paper somewhere else.
- **Common confusion:** Trending is not the same as reviewed or cited. A paper on this list can still be unreviewed.
- **Exercise:** Pick one trending paper. Find its arXiv ID, then check it on Semantic Scholar.
