# Mistakes

> [!tldr] Errors worth keeping
> Log each wrong answer with the concept it belongs to. Correct it yourself first, then turn it into a card with a source.

## How to use

1. Log a mistake: date, concept link, what you answered, what was right, why you went wrong.
2. Write the correction without looking. The `mistake-review` skill checks it against the source and drafts the card; you edit it.
3. Each card ends with a link to its source note. `check_cards.py` fails a card without one and a question that appears twice in the vault.

## Mistake log

Format of one entry: `YYYY-MM-DD, [[Concept_Name|Concept name]]: I answered X. The answer is Y. I went wrong because Z.`
