# Misconceptions

> [!tldr] Confusions worth keeping
> Log the ideas you keep mixing up, then practise telling them apart. A confusion is not fixed by repeating the same card.

## How to use

1. Log a confusion: date, objective, what you answered, what the right answer is, and the two ideas you are mixing. One entry per pair.
2. Write two items that look alike, one where each idea applies. Say which one applies and why, before seeing the key.
3. Close a confusion only after you tell the two apart, without help, more than once.

## Confusion log

Format of one entry: `YYYY-MM-DD, [[Objective_id|Objective]]: I answered X, but it is Y. I mix up X and Y. Open.`
