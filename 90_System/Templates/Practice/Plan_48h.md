# Plan for the last 48 hours

A plan for the two days before an exam or interview. Timings are practical choices, not tested values; adjust them to your day.

| When | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1, first hour | Pretest: answer the likely questions cold, before reviewing | Guessing first improves learning of what was asked |
| Day 1 | Retrieval rounds on the weakest topics; mix confusable pairs | Testing and interleaving beat rereading |
| Day 1, evening | Short card review, then sleep | Spaced sessions and sleep consolidate |
| Day 2, at the hour of the real event | Observed spoken mock, then a debrief | Being watched changes performance; rehearse it |
| Day 2, afternoon | Fix the gaps from the mock with retrieval, not rereading | Feedback works when you act on it |
| Night before | No new material; light review only; sleep | New material late adds confusion, not memory |
| Just before | Reappraise nerves as excitement; read your one-page summary | Calmer start, quick cue for retrieval |
